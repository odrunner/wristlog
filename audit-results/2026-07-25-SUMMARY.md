## The two that matter most

**#1** is one line to close and nothing calls it. The sibling function
`commit_watch_fact_srv` in the very same migration is correctly locked to
`service_role`; the client-facing twin was simply never revoked after `fe17a2e`
moved the commit server-side. Impact is content poisoning of a shared surface, not
XSS (`escHtml` holds) or data theft — but it is the same class as last cycle's
`auto-add-brand` finding, and unlike that one the text is not even model-generated.

**#2** is the finding with the longest fuse. Nothing is slow today — the keyset
query runs in 2.0 ms and Postgres is correct to seq-scan 2,486 rows. But 934 million
tuples read on `logs` shows how hot the path is, and infinite scroll multiplied the
query count per session against a table with a single index. The indexes cost under
1 MB and matter only at the crossover; the planner cannot switch to them if they
don't exist.

## Pattern worth noting

Three of the four Medium reliability findings are **the same defect fixed in one
consumer and left in another**:

- **#3** — `isWearEntry` was established as the single wear rule on 2026-07-19 and
  routed through seven client call sites; the new email segment re-implemented half
  of it from scratch.
- **#4** — `337340e` (today) discovered that ~29% of Pro V2 sessions lack `psBE=` and
  fixed the weekly review. `rollout-check.py`, which shares the marker and the
  corpus, was not touched. The measured undercount is 28.3%.
- **#5** — the app's suppression key was bumped; the four test-harness sites that
  mirror it were not.

This is the exact pattern the 2026-07-19 audit named in its own self-assessment:
*"verified against the specific reported symptom rather than against the class of
problem — one call site instead of all consumers."* A grep for co-consumers before
committing would have caught all three.

### #5 uncovered three more real failures

Fixing the overlay revealed that the three "Post location" tests had been failing on
their own merits, masked as click-interception noise. Root cause: commit `9390b33`
("one-line occasion & location rows") trimmed `POST_LOCATION_PRESETS` to
`['Home', 'Work']` — **Travel stopped being a chip** — but the tests still asserted a
Travel chip and a Travel selection on edit-post prefill.

The product behaviour is correct (Travel now round-trips as free text). The tests were
stale. Updated to assert the shipped presets, plus a count assertion so a third chip
can't silently reappear and break the one-line row, and a new test covering chip
selection for a value that *is* a preset. This is the concrete cost of the red suite
being written off as flaky: three genuine stale-coverage failures sat invisible behind it.

### The test intermittent: root cause found

Reproduced under `--repeat-each`. `openEditPost()` silently no-ops when the in-memory
`logs` array hasn't loaded yet — `waitForAppBoot()` only waits for the nav — so the
modal stays hidden. A **third** edit-post test still carried the race after the two
fixed on 2026-07-25. All three now wait via `waitForLog()`. Group stress: **56/56**,
previously failing ~1 in 21.

**Honest limit:** the originally-observed failure was
*"typing free text clears any chip selection"*, which did **not** reproduce in 30
isolated runs or in the stressed group, and exercises a different path (the new-post
composer, no `openEditPost`). It may or may not share this cause. Not claimed fixed.
