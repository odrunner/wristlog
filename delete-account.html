Delete Your WRotate Account

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You can permanently delete your WRotate account and all associated data at any time, directly from the app or the website.

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to WRotate (the Android or iOS app, or wrotate.com).
  2. Open your profile and go to Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

If you can no longer sign in, email privacy@wrotate.com from the address associated with your account and we will delete it for you.

What is deleted

Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes:

  • Your profile, display name, and photo
  • Your watch collection, wishlist, wear logs, and straps
  • Your posts, comments, likes, and club memberships
  • Your uploaded photos
  • Your measurement sessions and results
  • Your notification device tokens and preferences

What is kept

No personal data is retained after deletion. Aggregate statistics that cannot identify you (for example, anonymous measurement accuracy metrics used to improve the timegrapher) are not linked to deleted accounts. Standard server logs expire on their own schedule within days.

Questions
