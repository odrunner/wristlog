Help & Guide
Welcome to WristLog. This guide covers everything you need to know to get the most out of the app. Your watch data is stored privately in your own browser — nothing is sent to any server.
Found a bug? Have an idea? Let us know 👇
📦 Building Your Collection
1
Go to Collection → "+ Add Watch"
This opens the add watch form. You can fill everything manually, or use the URL shortcut below.
2
Paste the manufacturer's product page URL and tap 🔍 Fetch
Go to the brand's website (e.g. rolex.com, omegawatches.com, seikowatches.com), open the exact watch page, copy the URL from your browser, and paste it into the URL field. Tap Fetch — the app fills in brand, name, reference number, and often pulls a photo automatically.
3
Fill in price, purchase date, and condition
These power the Cost-per-Wear metric in Reports. Purchase date is also used to sort your collection.
4
Add straps if the watch has more than one
Expand the ⌚ Straps section. Add each strap with a name (e.g. "NATO Black", "Oyster Bracelet"). Toggle which one is currently on the watch — the app will ask which strap you wore each time you log.
💡 Best URL sources: Brand websites, Hodinkee shop, WatchCharts listings — these tend to auto-fill the most fields. Avoid marketplace URLs (eBay, Chrono24) as they rarely work with Fetch.
⌚ Logging a Wear
1
Go to the Track tab
The date defaults to today. You can tap any past date on the calendar to log retroactively.
2
Select the watch you wore
Tap the watch from the list. If it has multiple straps, a strap selector appears — pick which one was on the watch.
3
Choose a use case (optional)
Work, Leisure, Dinner, or Travel. This lets you see wear patterns in Reports — e.g. which watch you reach for on weekdays vs. weekends.
4
Add notes and tap "Log This Wear"
Notes are optional — great for occasion details ("Wedding of John & Sara"), outfit pairings, or quick impressions.
💡 Already logged but picked the wrong watch or use case? Tap the ✎ pencil icon in the Wear History table to edit any past entry.
📦 Collection Features
🎮 Ranking Game
Tap 🎮 Rank in the Collection header. You'll see head-to-head match-ups — tap the watch you prefer within 3 seconds. After all rounds, save your ranking to sort the collection by personal preference. Elo scores accumulate over multiple sessions, so the ranking gets more accurate over time.
🏷 Tag Filtering
Add tags to watches when editing (Chronograph, Dress Watch, Diver, etc.). Tag chips appear above the collection grid — tap one to filter. Tap again to clear.
📈 Market Prices
Paste a WatchCharts URL when editing a watch, then tap 🔄 Update All Prices in the Collection header to pull current market values. The app tracks price history so you can see how values trend over time.
🌟 Wishlist
1
Add watches you want to buy
Paste the manufacturer or listing URL and Fetch auto-fills the details. Track asking price vs. current market price to spot the right moment to buy.
2
Move to Collection when purchased
Edit the wishlist item and tap "Move to Collection" — it transfers all the details so you don't have to re-enter anything.
📊 Reports
—
Wear frequency & streaks
See how often you wear each watch, your longest streak, and which days of the week you wear each piece most.
—
Cost per wear
Divides the purchase price by total wears. A great way to see which watches deliver the most value per outing.
—
Year in Review
A full-year heat-map calendar showing every day you logged a watch. Scroll to the bottom of the Reports page and select the year.
💾 Backup & Restore
↑
Export — download a backup
Tap ↑ Export in the top-right header. Saves a .json file with all your watches and logs. Keep a copy before clearing browser data or switching devices.
↓
Import — restore a backup
Tap ↓ Import and select a previously exported .json file. This merges the backup with any existing data — watches and logs are matched by ID, so there are no duplicates.
💡 Switching phones? Export on the old phone, open the app on the new phone, then Import the file. All your data transfers instantly.
