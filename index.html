Help & Guide
Welcome to WRotate. Track every wear, build your collection story, and share with other enthusiasts. Sign in with Google to sync your data securely — your collection is available on any device.
Found a bug? Have an idea? Let us know 👇
📦 Building Your Collection
1
Go to Collection → "+ Add Watch"
Opens the add watch form. Fill it manually or use the URL fetch shortcut below.
2
Paste a product page URL and tap 🔍 Fetch
Go to the brand's website (rolex.com, omegawatches.com, seikowatches.com…), open the exact watch page, copy the URL, and paste it in. Fetch fills in brand, name, reference number, and often pulls a photo automatically.
3
Fill in price, purchase date, and condition
These power the Cost-per-Wear metric in Reports. Purchase date is also used to sort your collection.
4
Add straps if the watch has more than one
Expand the ⌚ Straps section. Add each strap (e.g. "NATO Black", "Oyster Bracelet") and toggle which is currently on the watch — the app will ask which strap you wore each time you log.
💡 Best URL sources: Brand websites, Hodinkee, WatchCharts — these auto-fill the most fields. Avoid marketplace URLs (eBay, Chrono24) as they rarely work with Fetch.
⌚ Logging a Wear
1
Go to the Track tab
The date defaults to today. Use the date field to log retroactively. The ⭐ Today's Pick card suggests a watch based on your rotation — tap ⌚ Log This Wear to log it directly.
2
Select the watch you wore
Tap the watch from the grid. If it has multiple straps, a strap picker appears — choose which one was on the watch.
3
Choose a use case and add notes (optional)
Work, Leisure, Dinner, or Travel — use cases power the wear-pattern charts in Reports. Notes are great for occasion details, outfit pairings, or quick impressions.
4
Add a photo and set visibility, then tap "Log This Wear"
Each log can be 🌐 Public (visible to all followers), 👥 Friends (only friends see it), or 🔒 Private (only you). The default is set in your Privacy settings.
💡 Logged the wrong watch or use case? Tap the ✎ pencil icon on any feed card or in the Wear History table to edit it.
✨ Collection Features
🎮 Ranking Game
Tap 🎮 Rank in the Collection header for head-to-head match-ups. Tap the watch you prefer within 3 seconds. Elo scores accumulate over sessions so your ranking gets more accurate over time.
🏷 Tag Filtering
Add tags when editing a watch (Chronograph, Dress Watch, Diver…). Tag chips appear above the grid — tap one to filter, tap again to clear.
📈 Market Prices
Paste a WatchCharts URL when editing a watch, then tap 🔄 Update All Prices in the Collection header to pull current market values. Price history is tracked over time.
🔒 Per-Watch Privacy
On your profile page, tap any watch in your Collection showcase to cycle its privacy: Public → Friends Only → Private. Private watches are hidden from everyone else.
🌟 Wishlist
1
Add watches you want to buy
Paste a URL and Fetch auto-fills the details. Track asking price vs. current market price to spot the right moment to buy.
2
Drag to reorder your priority ranking
Grab the ⠿ handle and drag items up or down. The #1 spot is highlighted in gold.
3
Share your wishlist with friends
Go to your profile → Privacy section → set Wishlist to 👥 Friends or 🌐 Public. Friends can then see your wishlist on your profile — great for gift inspiration or comparing GAS lists.
4
Move to Collection when purchased
Edit the wishlist item and tap "Move to Collection" — all details transfer so you don't have to re-enter anything.
🏠 Feed & Community
1
Follow or friend other collectors
Tap 🔍 Find People in the Feed header. For Public profiles tap Follow — their logs appear in your feed immediately. For Friends Only profiles tap Add Friend and wait for them to accept — once accepted you can see their collection and wishlist (if shared).
2
Post a standalone update
Tap ✏ Post in the Feed header to share a photo or text update that isn't tied to a specific wear log — a wrist shot, a quick thought, or a new arrival.
3
Like and comment on posts
Tap ❤️ to like any post. Tap 💬 to read and leave comments. You'll get a 🔔 notification when someone likes or comments on your posts.
4
Notifications
Tap the 🔔 bell in the top-right corner to see likes, comments, and friend requests. Tapping a like or comment notification opens the Feed. Tapping a friend request notification lets you accept or decline.
💡 To unfollow a public profile, go to their profile and tap Following → it turns red and asks Unfollow? — tap again to confirm. To remove a friend, tap Friends ✓ → Unfriend?.
👤 Profile & Privacy
1
Edit your profile
Tap your avatar in the top-right to open your profile, then tap the ✏️ icon to edit your display name, @username, bio, and profile photo.
2
Privacy section — always visible on your profile
Below your edit form you'll find a Privacy card with pill selectors for three settings. Changes save instantly — no Save button needed.
3
Profile visibility
🌐 Public — anyone can view your profile and follow you. 👥 Friends Only — others must send a friend request; only accepted friends see your full profile. 🔒 Private — hidden from search; only existing connections see you. Changing visibility does not remove existing followers.
4
Collection & Wishlist visibility
Set each independently: 🌐 Public shows it to anyone who views your profile, 👥 Friends shows it only to accepted friends, 🔒 Private keeps it hidden from everyone. Wishlist is private by default.
5
Default Post Visibility
Also in the Privacy section — new wear logs and posts default to this visibility. You can override it per post when logging.
📊 Reports

Wear frequency & streaks
See how often you wear each watch, your longest streak, and which days of the week you reach for each piece most.

Purchase price divided by total wears — see which watches deliver the most value per outing.
—

A full-year heat-map calendar showing every day you logged a watch. Scroll to the bottom of Reports and select the year.
