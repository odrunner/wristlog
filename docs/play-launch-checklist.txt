[ ] App name:

[ ] Short description (80 chars max):

[ ] Full description (paste as-is, edit freely):
WRotate turns your phone into a watch accuracy tool and keeps your whole
collection in one place.

MEASURE LIKE A TIMEGRAPHER
Place your watch on the phone's microphone and WRotate listens to the
movement's ticks. You get rate (seconds per day), beat error, and amplitude
in about 17 seconds - the numbers a professional timegrapher gives you.
Audio is processed on your device in real time and never recorded or
uploaded.

YOUR COLLECTION
Add watches from a photo - WRotate identifies the brand and model for you.
Track specs, references, straps, and value estimates. Keep a wishlist.

WEAR TRACKING
Log what you wear each day and see which watches actually get wrist time,
with stats and streaks over any period.

SHARE IT (IF YOU WANT)
Post wrist shots to the feed, follow other collectors, or keep everything
private. Visibility is per post: public, followers, or close friends.

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOU
Optional notifications for wear logging and re-measuring a watch after a
few weeks - one tap to turn off.

Free to use. Sign in with Google or Apple.
